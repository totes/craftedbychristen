My Teaching Philosophy

Every person is creative. My job isn't to teach you how to be an artist—it's to help you discover that you already are one.

I believe art should be joyful, accessible, and personal. In my classes, there are no mistakes—only happy discoveries. Whether you're five or seventy-five, picking up a brush for the first time or the thousandth, you belong here.
