Art in the Classroom

Christen is a dedicated art teacher in St. Charles Parish, Louisiana, where she brings creativity and self-expression into the lives of her students every day.

Through hands-on projects, thoughtful curriculum, and genuine encouragement, she helps young artists discover their creative voices and build confidence through art.

From painting and drawing to mixed media and beyond, her classroom is a space where every student is an artist.

What Students Experience

Hands-On Learning

Students explore a wide range of mediums and techniques, building real skills through projects they're proud of.

Creative Confidence

A welcoming environment where students feel safe to take risks, make mistakes, and grow as artists and individuals.

Celebrating Every Voice

Each student brings something unique to the table. Art class is a place to celebrate individuality and self-expression.

Teaching Philosophy

Every child is creative. My job isn't to teach them how to be artists—it's to help them discover that they already are.

I believe art should be joyful, accessible, and personal. In my classroom, there are no mistakes—only happy discoveries. Every student who walks through the door belongs here, and I want them to know it.
